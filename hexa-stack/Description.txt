Hexa Stack
Find your flow in the most satisfying puzzle game ever
Welcome to Hexa Stack, a perfect blend of brain-teasing challenges and relaxing ASMR experiences. The goal is simple but addictive: organize the chaos. Drag and drop piles of colorful hexagonal tiles onto the board, watch them shuffle and merge with their neighbors, and clear the screen with satisfying "clicks."
Whether you want to sharpen your mind with logic puzzles or simply unwind after a long day, this game offers the perfect escape. Can you master the art of sorting and keep your board clean?
Key Features of Hexa Stack
Stimulating Brain Training: Keep your mind sharp! Plan your moves carefully, manage limited space on the grid, and develop strategies to solve increasingly complex sorting puzzles.
Hypnotic ASMR Experience: Immerse yourself in pure satisfaction. Enjoy the soothing sounds of tiles clicking, the smooth visual animations of shuffling stacks, and the tactile feedback that melts stress away.
Vibrant Minimalist Design: Feast your eyes on a clean, modern aesthetic. Enjoy a palette of pleasing colors and high-quality 3D graphics that make every move feel delightful.
Endless Progression: The fun never stops. Journey through hundreds of unique levels, from easy warm-ups to tricky brain-busters that will test your logic skills to the limit.
Power-ups & Tools: Stuck on a hard level? Use the Hammer to smash unwanted tiles or the Undo button to fix mistakes, keeping your gameplay smooth and frustration-free.
How to play
Place: Drag tile stacks onto the grid.
Match: Place same-colored tiles next to each other to merge.
Clear: Complete a full stack to remove it and score points.
