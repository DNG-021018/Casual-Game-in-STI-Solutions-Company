Night Escape
Darkness is your only friend. Can you survive the night?
Welcome to Night Escape, the ultimate test of stealth and strategy. You are a stickman prisoner stuck in a high-security facility. The plan is simple: wait for nightfall, dodge the patrols, and find your way to freedom.
But escape won't be easy. The prison is filled with security cameras, laser sensors, and ruthless guards who never sleep. You must move silently, hide in the shadows, and solve tricky puzzles to unlock the gates. One wrong step, and the alarm will trigger!
Are you smart enough to ghost through the security, or will you be caught in the spotlight?
Key Features of Night 
Intense Stealth Action: Feel the thrill of the escape! Time your movements perfectly to slip past rotating cameras and patrolling guards. The tension rises with every step.
Brain-Teasing Levels: Each floor is a unique puzzle. Analyze the patrol patterns, disable traps, and find the safest route to the exit.
Night Mode Atmosphere: Immerse yourself in a stylish dark environment where lighting and shadows play a crucial role in your survival.
Smooth Controls: Designed for mobile, the simple one-finger control makes it easy to sneak around but hard to master the perfect run.
How to play
Move Silently: Drag to move your stickman. Stay out of the guards' red vision cones.
Hide in Shadows: Use corners and dark spots to avoid detection by CCTV cameras.
Find the Exit: Collect keys, hack computers, and open doors to progress to the next level.
